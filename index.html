Privacy Policy

1. About this policy

Paragon Lite (“Paragon Lite,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an academic productivity application designed to help students and lecturers organize courses, learning resources, academic tasks and communication. This policy applies to the app and related services that link to it.

By using Paragon Lite, you acknowledge that information may be handled as described below. If you do not agree, please stop using the app.

2. Information we handle

The information involved depends on which features you choose to use. It may include:

  • Account information: details you provide when registering or managing your account, such as your name, email address, profile details and authentication-related records.
  • Academic content: courses you create or join, course details, notes, learning resources, uploaded or submitted files, assignments, study materials and progress information you choose to enter.
  • Messages and user content: messages, group or course-chat content and other information you send through communication features.
  • AI feature inputs: prompts, questions, text or files you submit to AI-powered features, together with outputs generated in response.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: information needed to operate, protect, troubleshoot and improve the service, which may include device, app, log, error and usage information depending on the feature and platform.
  • Subscription and purchase information: if you use paid features, information needed to associate a purchase with your account and provide premium access, such as product or plan selected, purchase or transaction identifiers, subscription status, entitlement status, renewal or expiration information, and purchase history supplied by the relevant platform or billing service. Payment card details are generally handled by the app store or payment provider, not directly by Paragon Lite.
  • Support communications: information you provide if you contact us for assistance.

Please avoid uploading sensitive personal information or material you are not authorized to share. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or other secrets in prompts or course content.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • create and maintain accounts and provide app features;
  • save courses, resources, progress and content you choose to keep in the app;
  • enable messaging and collaboration features;
  • process requests submitted to AI features and return responses;
  • secure the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors and maintain reliability;
  • respond to support requests and communicate important service or policy updates; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. AI-powered features

If you use an AI feature, the text, questions, files or other inputs you submit may be sent to the relevant AI service provider to generate a response. Avoid including personal or confidential information unless you have the right to provide it and are comfortable with the processing needed for that feature.

AI outputs can be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed before being relied on for academic, professional or other important decisions. AI-generated content does not replace your own judgment or applicable academic-integrity rules. The data handling practices of third-party AI providers may also apply when their services process your inputs.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not publish your private account content for general access. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate Paragon Lite, such as authentication, database/hosting, storage, diagnostics or AI infrastructure providers, where those services are used by the feature you choose.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users or the service, investigate abuse, or establish or defend legal rights. If the service or its assets are reorganized or transferred, information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

Features that you use to share content—such as course spaces or chats—make that content available to the people included in or granted access to those spaces. Check recipients and course access before sharing.

6. Subscriptions, purchases and premium access

Paragon Lite may offer optional paid subscriptions or other paid access to premium features. When these features are available and you choose to purchase them, purchase and subscription information may be processed by Google Play or another relevant app store and by RevenueCat, a subscription-management service used to help validate purchases, associate entitlements with an app account, and manage subscription status. The services involved depend on the platform and the purchase flow available to you.

Payment processing, billing details, renewals, cancellations and refunds are subject to the relevant store or payment provider terms and settings. Paragon Lite does not need to receive your full payment-card number to grant premium access. The app may receive subscription status and entitlement information so it can determine which features your account can use. We do not control the independent privacy practices of Google Play, RevenueCat or other third-party providers; review their policies for further information.

Plan availability, price, billing period, trial terms (if any), renewal conditions and cancellation instructions should be reviewed on the purchase screen before you confirm a transaction. Unless the purchase screen or applicable store terms say otherwise, a subscription may renew automatically until cancelled through the relevant store. Uninstalling Paragon Lite does not itself cancel a subscription.

7. Storage, security and retention

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, but no app, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Access controls and security protections may vary by feature and the underlying service provider.

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain your account and content, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet legal obligations. Retention may depend on the type of information and the settings or services involved. Deleting the app from your device may not delete an account or information stored by online services.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate measures should be used for such transfers. Data-protection laws and available rights can differ by location.

9. Account deletion requests

You can request deletion of your Paragon Lite account and associated personal information through our Account Deletion Request page. Submit the form with the information needed to identify your account. We may contact you to verify your identity before processing the request. We aim to process verified requests within 30 days, subject to applicable law and any information we are required or permitted to retain. Account deletion does not automatically cancel an active subscription; manage or cancel subscriptions through the relevant app store.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You can limit the information you provide and stop using optional features. To make a privacy request or ask about account deletion, contact us using the address below and include enough detail for us to understand your request. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some information may need to be retained where law permits or requires it.

11. Children

Paragon Lite is intended for a general student and lecturer audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

12. Third-party services and links

The app may rely on or link to services operated by other organizations. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage you to review those policies when using a third-party feature or link.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app, its features or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use after an update takes effect means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.
